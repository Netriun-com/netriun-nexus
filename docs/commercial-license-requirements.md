# Commercial license technical requirements

Status: product/engineering input for qualified legal counsel. This document
is **not legal advice, a commercial license, terms of service, or an offer**.
No final legal wording is selected here.

A future Netriun Enterprise agreement should unambiguously define:

- the customer/legal entity and covered affiliates;
- permitted self-hosted, production, staging, development and DR deployments;
- how many installations and concurrent active clones are permitted;
- whether an installation identity may be transferred and the reissue process;
- human-identity, workspace and cloud-account limits and measurement rules;
- entitled features and whether limits are hard, contractual, or both;
- license start, `not_before`, expiry, renewal and the 14-day technical grace
  behavior;
- non-destructive expiry behavior and continued access/export of customer data;
- backup, restore, node replacement, DR tests, failover and temporary overlap;
- restrictions and remedies for VM, namespace, database and full-environment
  clones without describing them as a technical security guarantee;
- support scope, response targets, maintenance windows and any SLA/service
  credits;
- updates, security fixes, supported versions and end-of-support;
- privacy, telemetry and customer-data handling, including the offline nature
  of license verification;
- audit/evidence rights if commercially required, with scope, notice,
  confidentiality and frequency limits;
- suspension/termination, cure periods, effect on Enterprise capabilities and
  the requirement that customer data remain readable/exportable;
- warranties, liability, indemnity, governing law and dispute terms for counsel;
- relationship to the AGPL Community/Core license, trademarks and proprietary
  Enterprise/Cloud components.

The signed technical license file is an enforcement artifact, not the whole
commercial agreement. It must not silently grant or remove rights that are not
represented in the approved order/agreement.
